## About

PVZ meets Stardew Valley!

You are a space farmer growing and selling crops across multiple planets, using rifts to expand your interplanetary farming network while defending against munchers, alien pests that vary between planets.

As profits grow and debt looms, you answer to the Agricultural Space Society and its CEO, “The Man,” a mysterious trillionaire whose true nature remains hidden.

Good luck space pioneer!

Play [here](https://official-itchio.itch.io/stellar-harvest?secret=JedGTY65TFiabTqDBDnYhtORz2E)!

## Controls
- WASD to move
- Left mouse button to interact
- E to open inventory
- Esc to pause
